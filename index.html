      About Meteo

      Reading the forecast

      A sample forecast with every option on. The numbers match the list below.

        1. 1
          Summary

          When rain starts and its heaviest hour, then the temperature range. With the wind setting, the strongest wind and gusts from 50 km/h. With the sky setting, an icon shows the current sky.

        2. 2
          Rain amount

          Rain or snow expected in that hour (mm). Shown from 0.1 mm.

        3. 3
          Rain bar

          Grows with the rain amount. A full bar is the wettest hour, or 2 mm on drier days.

        4. 4
          Chance of rain

          Probability of more than 0.1 mm of rain or snow in that hour.

        5. 5
          Hour

          Start of each hour; the first column, the current hour, reads “Now”. Phones label every second hour.

        6. 6
          Sky

          Clear, partly cloudy, cloudy, fog, rain, snow or thunder; clear skies get night icons after dark. Needs the sky setting and a tall enough screen.

        7. 7
          Temperature

          Air temperature at the start of each hour, in °C.

        8. 8
          Wind

          Wind speed 10 m above the ground at the start of each hour, in km/h. Needs the wind setting and a tall enough screen.

        9. 9
          Place

          The searched place, or your current location with its town when found.

        10. 10
          Update time

          When the forecast was fetched. Returning to the app refreshes it once it is 15 minutes old.

        Data sources

        • Open-Meteo Weather Forecast API

          Hourly temperature, rain and chance of rain from national weather services (CC BY 4.0).

        • Open-Meteo Geocoding API

          Place and postal code search based on GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

        • OpenStreetMap Nominatim

          Name of the current location from OpenStreetMap data (ODbL).
